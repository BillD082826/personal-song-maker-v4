CAPTION FILE
------------

LyriBop_Ad_Captions.md

Each numbered ad has TWO captions in this file:

1. Instagram
2. Facebook

Always match the caption number to the picture number.


HOW TO POST ON INSTAGRAM
------------------------

1. Choose one of the 7 LyriBop ad pictures.

2. Remember its number.
   Example:
   LyriBop_Ad_03_Gratitude.png = Ad #3

3. Open LyriBop_Ad_Captions.md.

4. Find the matching ad number.

5. Find the Instagram section underneath that ad.

6. Copy the complete Instagram caption.

7. Create a new Instagram post.

8. Select the matching LyriBop picture.

9. Paste the Instagram caption.

10. Review the picture and caption before publishing.

11. Publish the post.

The Instagram captions tell customers:
"Tap the link in our profile."


HOW TO POST ON FACEBOOK
-----------------------

1. Choose one of the 7 LyriBop ad pictures.

2. Remember its number.

3. Open LyriBop_Ad_Captions.md.

4. Find the matching ad number.

5. Find the Facebook section underneath that ad.

6. Copy the complete Facebook caption.

7. Create a new post on the LyriBop Facebook Page.

8. Select the matching LyriBop picture.

9. Paste the Facebook caption.

10. Review the picture and caption before publishing.

11. Publish the post.

The Facebook captions tell customers:
"Click the Learn More button on our page."


IMPORTANT
---------

Do not delete or move the original pictures when posting them.

Do not delete the master caption file.

Uploading a picture to Facebook or Instagram does NOT remove it from this folder.

Copying a caption does NOT remove it from the caption file.

These ads can be reused in the future.

You do not have to post the ads in numerical order.
