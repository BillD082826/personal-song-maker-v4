# Using the LyriBop scheduler

Updated November 7, 2026. Facebook and Instagram are prepared but remain OFF.

1. Open the hosted Ad Scheduler from the advertising dashboard.
2. Choose an ad, platform and Eastern date/time. You may use a different ad in each slot, up to three daily slots per platform over seven days. Save the plan.
3. In the 7-Day Planner, review the artwork, caption, destination and time, then explicitly approve the posts you intend to send. Saving a plan alone does not approve it.
4. In Scheduler Controls, review each platform’s waiting queue and readiness messages. Start Facebook or Instagram separately only when ready. Starting also makes later approvals eligible while that platform remains ON.
5. Use Posting Report to check verified results and open the published post. A saved plan or approval is not proof of publication.
6. Stop a platform when you want to prevent new sends. An already-running send may finish. Saved plans, approvals and history remain.

## When something needs attention

- Status unavailable: refresh before changing a switch. An unreadable status does not mean OFF.
- Connection expired or unavailable: review Connections before restarting. Do not assume a failed login means a post failed.
- Delivery needs review: check Posting Report and the platform for an existing post. Do not create a replacement or retry an uncertain delivery until resolved.
- Time elapsed or content changed: review the plan again; the scheduler does not silently backfill missed posts.
- Planner paused: review the queue before resuming. Platform switches are separate from planner pause.

## Current limits

Single-image ads are supported. Story collections and videos need their own publishing verification. Pinterest board selection saves draft information; public scheduled Pinterest publishing is not implemented, and Standard access has not been verified. Weekly performance summaries and promotional tokens remain separate backlog items.
