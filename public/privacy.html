YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 23, 2026

LyriBop is operated personally by Bill D’Onofrio in Massachusetts, United States. We create personalized songs from the stories and details customers provide. This policy explains how information is handled through our website, previews, orders, communications and reviews.

Questions or privacy requests?
Email LyriBop@gmail.com. Include your order number if relevant. Do not send passwords, full payment-card details or identification documents.

Information we collect

We collect information you submit, including your name, email address, recipient name, occasion, music preferences, stories, memories and special messages. We keep associated order details, referral information, payment status, generated lyrics and audio, selected versions and delivery information. If you submit a review, we collect the rating, review text and optional display name. We also receive information you send when contacting us.

Please provide only information needed for your song. Avoid highly sensitive information, and make sure you have an appropriate basis for sharing information about someone else.

How we use information

We use information to create and revise songs and previews, manage orders and referrals, process payments, deliver songs, send order messages, answer questions, administer reviews, maintain records, troubleshoot problems and protect the service.

Services used to provide your song

  • OpenAI: processes song instructions, including submitted stories, recipient details, preferences and messages, to generate lyrics.
  • ElevenLabs: processes music instructions and song material to generate audio.
  • PayPal: processes payments, including eligible Venmo and card methods. LyriBop does not collect or store your full payment-card number, card security code, or PayPal/Venmo password. We retain payment transaction identifiers and status to manage your order. The checkout uses PayPal’s payment interface.
  • Resend: processes email addresses and message content to send LyriBop emails.
  • Hosting, database and backup services: support our website and storage of order information and generated files. Our website is hosted on Render.

These providers process information under their applicable terms and privacy practices. Information may be processed in the United States and other locations where providers operate. We may also disclose information when required by applicable law or valid legal process, or when necessary to protect rights and the service.

LyriBop does not sell customer information or share it with other businesses for their own advertising.

Browser storage and technical information

The ordering page uses browser session storage to restore preview and checkout progress. Our service and hosting providers process technical information needed to deliver requests and protect the service, which may include IP addresses, browser information, request times and diagnostic information. Payment services may use their own cookies or similar technologies. Your browser settings control browser storage; clearing it may interrupt saved checkout progress.

Email choices

LyriBop may send promotional emails. You can stop future marketing emails through the unsubscribe link in those messages or contact us at LyriBop@gmail.com. Marketing unsubscribe does not stop order confirmations or song-delivery messages.

Reviews and private song links

When the review form explains public display and you submit a review, your review, rating and chosen display name may appear on the LyriBop website. A name is preferred but optional. Leave it blank to appear as Anonymous. Anonymous public display does not remove the internal link between your review and order.

Submitting a review does not grant permission to publish your private song or story. We will seek separate permission before using those materials publicly. Older reviews will not be treated as having agreed to a notice added after they were submitted.

Preview and delivery links provide access to the associated song or page. Anyone with a working link may be able to access that content. Keep your link private unless you intend to share it.

Retention and deletion requests

We currently have no scheduled deletion period for customer orders and songs. This does not guarantee that files will remain available permanently. There is no customer-facing delete button.

You may request access, correction or deletion by emailing LyriBop@gmail.com. We may ask for information needed to verify your request. We will review requests under applicable law. Some records may need to be retained for legal obligations or payment disputes, and backup or provider copies may have different removal timelines. Deletion can make your song and delivery link unavailable.

Audience and information about children

LyriBop is intended for adults ordering personalized songs, including gifts for children. We do not intentionally solicit personal information directly from children under 13. If you believe a child has submitted personal information directly to us, contact LyriBop@gmail.com so we can review and address it.

Business social-media reporting

We review performance information for LyriBop’s own social-media content, such as views, impressions, clicks, saves and interactions, to understand our marketing. If we connect business accounts through an authorized platform integration, we use the information made available for that reporting purpose and handle it under the platform’s applicable terms. Customers do not need to supply social-media passwords or connect their personal accounts to order a song.

Security and updates

Our application uses restricted administration, token-based delivery links and request-limiting measures. No system can guarantee complete security. We will update this policy when our practices change and provide additional notice where required.
