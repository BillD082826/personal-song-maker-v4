What would make the song feel like yours?

Start with their name and a memory you share. It could be your first date, a place you return to, a road trip, or a little habit that still makes you smile.

You do not need to write lyrics. A few clear details give the song something personal to build on. Say what happened and why it matters to you.

A few ideas to help you start

  • Where did you meet, and what do you remember about that day?
  • Is there a nickname, saying, or shared joke you would like included?
  • What do you appreciate about the life you have built together?
  • What would you like them to hear from you on this anniversary?

Choose a music style, lead voice, and mood. Add an optional Special Message to help shape the sung lyrics. The wording may be adapted to fit the song.
